5. ADVERTISING, IDENTIFIERS AND CONSENT

We show ads through CleverAdsSolutions (CAS) mediation and its partner networks. Advertising and analytics partners may access your advertising ID, device data and approximate location derived from your IP address in order to show and measure ads.

Where the law requires it, personalized ads are shown only with your consent. You can change your choice at any time in the game settings ("Data collection agreement"). Declining personalized ads does not remove ads; you will still see non-personalized ads.

On iOS we ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before accessing your advertising identifier. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad tracking in your device settings. Withdrawing consent stops future personalized advertising but does not affect processing that already took place.

8. DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

You can ask us to delete your account and data through the contact form on this website by choosing "Account" as the reason, or by emailing . Include your user ID, which is shown in the game settings and on the support screen.

  • If your account is linked to an email address, send the request from that address.
  • If your account is linked only to Facebook, include your Facebook ID.
  • If your account is linked only to Google or Apple, include your user ID and nickname.

We respond within 30 days. We delete your profile, nickname, progress, friends, linked sign-in IDs, push token, IP history and recovery code.

We keep a limited set of data where we are required or entitled to do so:

  • purchase and tax records for 5 years, as required by law;
  • if the account was banned for breaking the rules: a minimal ban record containing hashed account and device identifiers, the reason and the date, so that the ban cannot be evaded. The rest of the data is deleted;
  • aggregated statistics that no longer identify you.

Deletion cannot be undone: your progress and purchased items are lost.
